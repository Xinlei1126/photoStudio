01 / About

Mira Lind · Founder & lead photographer

Photography with feeling

Quiet moments.
Strong images.

Lumen & Frame is a fictional boutique photography studio founded in Stockholm in 2021 by Mira Lind. Mira started the studio after five years assisting commercial photographers and wanted to build a slower, more personal experience for clients.

Today, the studio works with couples, families, founders, makers and small creative teams. Every project begins with a short planning call, a simple visual brief and clear guidance so clients know exactly what to expect.

Our style combines soft natural light, documentary moments and editorial composition. Final galleries are delivered in a private online collection within 10–21 days, depending on the session.
